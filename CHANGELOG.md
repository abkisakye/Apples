## v1.0.0

Release tag: `v1.0.0`

Version 1.0.0 is the first complete Laravel replacement baseline for the legacy Access-based mini supermarket workflow.

Included in v1.0.0:

- sales, purchases, customer payments, and supplier payments
- stock balances, transfers, adjustments, movement history, and stock counts
- customer and supplier statements plus aging reports
- role-based access, user management, and permissions matrix
- dashboard summaries, follow-ups, and activity log
- printable receipts, invoices, statements, and stock documents
- capital input tracking
- Access import support and legacy migration utilities
- client demo, UAT, backup, and deployment support docs
