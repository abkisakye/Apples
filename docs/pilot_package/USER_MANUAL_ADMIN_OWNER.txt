APPLES OF GOLD SUPERMARKET/POS SYSTEM
USER MANUAL - ADMIN / OWNER

1. LOGIN

Open:

http://localhost:8000

Log in using the assigned admin or owner account. Change the default admin password immediately after first login.

2. DASHBOARD

Menu:

Overview > Dashboard

The dashboard summarizes posted business activity for the selected date range:

- Sales
- Purchases
- Expenses
- Collections
- Gross Profit
- Net Profit
- Sales Returns

Cards are clickable and open the matching detail/report page with date_from and date_to filters.

The dashboard uses business dates:

- sales.sale_date
- purchases.purchase_date
- expenses.expense_date
- sale_returns.return_date
- payment dates for customer collections

Voided, cancelled, or draft records should not be counted.

3. BUSINESS SETTINGS

Menu:

Admin > Business Settings

Update business name, contact details, TIN, currency, print footer wording, logo, approval PIN, and cashier discount limit.

4. USERS AND ROLES

Menus:

Admin > Users
Admin > Roles
Admin > Permissions

Use Users to create staff accounts. Use Roles and Permissions to control access.

Common roles:

- admin
- manager
- cashier
- stock_clerk
- guest

Do not give cashier users business setup permissions unless they should create setup records.

5. SETUP / MASTER DATA

Menu:

Setup

Supported setup pages:

- /setup/stores
- /setup/categories
- /setup/payment-modes
- /setup/capital-sources
- /setup/expense-categories

Use setup pages for full management: create, edit, search, activate, and deactivate records.

6. PRODUCTS

Menu:

Stock & Buying > Products

Use Products to create supermarket items and selling units. A product can have multiple units such as Piece, Bottle, Box, Carton, or Pack.

The product form supports quick product category creation for authorized users. Duplicate category names are blocked.

7. STOCK

Menu:

Stock & Buying > Stock

Stock pages include:

- Stock Balances
- Reorder / Low Stock
- Stock History
- Transfers
- Adjustments
- Counts

Stock balances are shown by product unit to avoid confusion between pack sizes.

8. REPORTS

Menu:

Finance > Reports

Reports include:

- Financial Summary
- Payment Methods
- Customer Aging
- Supplier Aging
- Cashier Performance
- Daily Closing

Use the same date range when comparing dashboard and reports.

9. PURCHASES

Menu:

Stock & Buying > Purchases

Use purchases to receive stock from suppliers. The purchase page is a receiving/invoice workflow, not a cashier sale screen.

Cash purchase:

- Enter amount paid now equal to total.
- The purchase is shown under Purchases.
- No separate Supplier Payment voucher is required.

Credit purchase:

- Enter amount paid now lower than total, or zero.
- Balance due remains open.
- Later use Supplier Payments to clear the balance.

10. SUPPLIER BALANCES

Menus:

Stock & Buying > Purchases
Stock & Buying > Supplier Payments
Reports > Supplier Aging

Use the Outstanding Purchases button on Supplier Payments to find posted purchases with balance_due greater than zero.

11. CUSTOMER BALANCES

Menus:

Sales Desk > Customers
Sales Desk > Customer Payments
Reports > Customer Aging

Credit sales and customer opening balances can be paid through Customer Payments.

12. EXPENSES

Menu:

Finance > Expenses

Record operating expenses using controlled expense categories and payment modes. Expenses reduce net profit.

13. BACKUPS AND GO-LIVE CHECKS

Run before updates and at daily closing:

php artisan ops:backup-database

Run readiness checks:

php artisan ops:go-live-check

Run tests before deployment:

php artisan test

14. DAILY OWNER REVIEW

Recommended daily checks:

1. Dashboard totals for the day.
2. Sales list and voided/corrected sales.
3. Cash shifts and counted cash.
4. Purchases and supplier balances.
5. Customer balances.
6. Expenses.
7. Low stock.
8. Backup file created.
