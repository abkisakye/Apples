APPLES OF GOLD SUPERMARKET/POS SYSTEM
USER MANUAL - CASHIER

1. LOGIN

Open:

http://localhost:8000

Enter your username/email and password. Do not share your password.

2. CHANGE PASSWORD

Menu:

Account > Change Password

Change your password the first time you receive an account or whenever instructed by the owner.

3. OPEN CASH SHIFT

Menu:

Sales Desk > Cash Shifts

Open a shift before selling if your account requires cash shift control.

Steps:

1. Click Open Cash Shift.
2. Enter opening balance.
3. Save.

If the system says a shift is already open, continue using the current shift or ask the manager.

4. CREATE SALE

Menu:

Sales Desk > Sales

Click New Sale or Create Sale.

The POS screen has:

- Left: Search and Products
- Middle: Selected Products
- Right: Checkout

5. SEARCH AND ADD PRODUCT

Use Search at the top left. Search by:

- Product name
- Code
- Barcode
- Part number

Click Add to add the item. Use Qty + and - controls to change quantity.

6. CHOOSE CUSTOMER

For normal cash supermarket sales, use Walk-in Customer.

For credit sales, choose a real customer account. Walk-in Customer should not be used for credit.

If the customer is missing, ask a manager or authorized user to create the customer.

7. CHOOSE PAYMENT MODE

Use the payment shortcut buttons:

- CASH
- MOBILE MONEY
- CARD
- CREDIT

The shortcut sets the payment mode/type for faster checkout.

8. PAID AMOUNT, CHANGE, AND BALANCE

Enter Paid amount.

The system shows:

- Total
- Paid
- Balance
- Change

Cash sale:

- Paid should be equal to or greater than Total.
- Change is given when Paid is greater than Total.

Credit sale:

- Customer must be a real customer.
- Balance remains due.

9. PRINT RECEIPT

After posting sale, open the sale print page and print the receipt.

For thermal printers:

- Use 80mm paper if available.
- Disable browser headers and footers if needed.

10. CUSTOMER PAYMENT

Menu:

Sales Desk > Customer Payments

Use this when a customer pays later for a credit sale or opening balance.

Steps:

1. Select customer.
2. Select outstanding sale or opening balance.
3. Enter amount.
4. Choose payment mode.
5. Record payment.
6. Print receipt if needed.

11. CLOSE SHIFT

Menu:

Sales Desk > Cash Shifts

At the end of day:

1. Open your active shift.
2. Click Close.
3. Enter counted cash.
4. Confirm difference.
5. Save.

12. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

- Do not sell on credit to Walk-in Customer.
- Do not skip payment mode for a paid sale.
- Do not enter quantity in the wrong unit, for example box instead of piece.
- Do not override price unless approved.
- Do not close another cashier's shift unless you have permission.
- Do not share your login.
- Confirm receipt total before handing receipt to customer.
