APPLES OF GOLD SUPERMARKET/POS SYSTEM
USER MANUAL - STOCK AND PURCHASES

1. CREATE SUPPLIER

Menu:

Stock & Buying > Suppliers

Create supplier before recording purchases. Enter supplier name and contact details where available.

2. CREATE PRODUCT

Menu:

Stock & Buying > Products

Create product with:

- Product Name
- Code / Barcode
- Category
- Supplier
- Reorder Level
- VAT
- Status

If product category is missing and you have permission, use the "+ New" category modal beside Category.

3. ADD UNITS / SELLING PACKS

In the product form, use Units & Selling Packs.

Examples:

- Piece
- Box
- Carton
- Bottle

Each unit should have:

- Conversion Factor
- Cost Price
- Selling Price
- Barcode
- Part Number
- Unit Status
- Default POS Unit where needed

4. RECORD PURCHASE

Menu:

Stock & Buying > Purchases

Click New Purchase.

Purchase page areas:

- Supplier & Invoice
- Find Items
- Incoming Items
- Purchase Summary

Steps:

1. Choose supplier.
2. Choose purchase date.
3. Choose store.
4. Enter supplier invoice number if available.
5. Choose payment mode if paying now.
6. Enter amount paid now.
7. Add credit period if buying on credit.
8. Search items by name, code, barcode, or part number.
9. Add incoming items.
10. Enter Qty Received and Buying Cost.
11. Confirm subtotal, paid now, and balance.
12. Click Record Purchase.

5. CASH PURCHASE VS CREDIT PURCHASE

Cash purchase:

- Amount paid now equals total.
- Balance due is zero.
- Purchase remains visible under Purchases.
- No separate Supplier Payment voucher is required.

Credit purchase:

- Amount paid now is less than total, or zero.
- Balance due remains open.
- Use Supplier Payments later.

6. SUPPLIER PAYMENT LATER

From Purchases:

1. Find credit purchase with balance due.
2. Click Record Payment.
3. Supplier and purchase should be preselected.
4. Enter amount.
5. Choose payment mode.
6. Record payment.

From Supplier Payments:

1. Open Supplier Payments.
2. Click Outstanding Purchases if needed.
3. Select supplier and purchase.
4. Record payment.

7. PARTIAL SUPPLIER PAYMENT

Partial payments are allowed.

Rules:

- Amount must be greater than zero.
- Amount must not exceed outstanding balance.
- Remaining balance stays open after partial payment.
- Full payment clears the purchase balance.

8. OUTSTANDING PURCHASES

Menu:

Stock & Buying > Supplier Payments

Click Outstanding Purchases to open Purchases filtered to posted purchases with balance_due greater than zero.

9. STOCK BALANCES

Menu:

Stock & Buying > Stock

Stock balances are shown by product unit. This prevents pack sizes from looking like duplicate products.

Example:

Product: GONJA CRISPS
Unit: Box

Product: GONJA CRISPS
Unit: Piece

10. STOCK ADJUSTMENTS

Use adjustments only for controlled corrections:

- Damaged stock
- Shrinkage
- Found stock
- Manual correction

Do not use adjustments for normal supplier purchases or customer sales.

11. STOCK TRANSFERS

Use transfers when moving stock between stores/locations.

Confirm:

- From store
- To store
- Product unit
- Quantity
- Remarks

12. STOCK COUNTS

Use stock counts for physical stocktaking.

Steps:

1. Create count.
2. Assign staff/section if needed.
3. Enter counted quantities.
4. Save draft while counting.
5. Post final count after verification.
6. Print count document if needed.

13. REORDER / LOW STOCK

Menu:

Stock & Buying > Stock > Reorder

Products with stock at or below reorder level appear here. Use this page to plan purchases.
