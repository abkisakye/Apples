8. SALES FLOW

SaleController creates posted sales with sale_date, customer, store, selected product units, payment mode, amount paid, balance due, and stock-out inventory transactions.

Cashier sales may require an open cash shift. Walk-in customer credit is blocked. Price/discount overrides can require sales.override permission or approval PIN.

Sale::posted() filters status = posted.

9. PURCHASE FLOW

PurchaseController records supplier stock receiving using purchase_date, supplier, store, supplier invoice, payment mode, amount paid, balance due, and purchase items.

Posted purchases increase stock with inventory transactions.

Cash purchases paid during purchase entry remain on Purchases and do not create a separate Supplier Payment voucher unless business logic is changed intentionally.

Purchase::posted() filters status = posted.

10. STOCK FLOW

Inventory is tracked through inventory_transactions and product units.

Sources include:

- Purchases
- Sales
- Returns
- Adjustments
- Transfers
- Stock counts

Stock balance pages display by product unit to distinguish pack sizes.

11. CUSTOMER PAYMENT FLOW

CustomerPaymentController records payments against credit sales or customer opening balances.

Payments require payment mode and cannot exceed outstanding balance. Posted payments reduce the related sale or opening balance.

12. SUPPLIER PAYMENT FLOW

SupplierPaymentController records payments posted later against credit purchases.

Create page supports:

- purchase_id preselection from Purchases row
- supplier_id preselection
- manual supplier/purchase selection

Payments require payment mode and cannot exceed purchase.balance_due. Partial payments reduce balance_due; full payments clear it.

13. REPORTING / DASHBOARD LOGIC

Dashboard uses selected date_from/date_to filters and business date columns:

- sales.sale_date
- purchases.purchase_date
- expenses.expense_date
- payment dates
- return dates

Gross profit is based on sale item revenue less cost_price_snapshot times quantity. Net profit is gross profit less expenses.

Collections include received money from sale-time payments and posted customer payments.

16. KNOWN LIMITATIONS / SUPPORT NOTES

- Offline installation requires vendor and public/build to already be included.
- MySQL backup requires mysqldump on PATH.
- Local http login can fail if SESSION_SECURE_COOKIE=true.
- Cash purchases paid during purchase entry are shown under Purchases, not Supplier Payments.
- Product stock balances are per product unit; repeated product names can be correct when units differ.
- Admin seeder production fallback can generate a random password if INITIAL_ADMIN_PASSWORD is not set.
