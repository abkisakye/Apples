Generated on 21 Apr 2026

## Welcome

This guide helps you review the new supermarket system in a simple way.

The purpose of this session is to help you see how the system can support daily work in your business.

## What The System Helps You Do

The system is designed to help a mini supermarket:

- make sales faster at the counter
- track stock coming in and going out
- manage purchases from suppliers
- track customer credit and payments
- print receipts, invoices, and statements
- monitor business performance through reports

## What To Look At First

Please go through the system in this simple order:

1. open the dashboard
2. review products and stock balances
3. create a purchase
4. create a sale
5. print a receipt or invoice
6. review customer or supplier statements
7. check the reports

## What To Notice During The Demo

As you use the system, focus on these questions:

- is the sales process fast and clear?
- is stock information easy to understand?
- are the receipts and statements presentable?
- can you quickly see what customers owe?
- can you quickly see what stock is low?
- does this feel better than the old Access workflow?
