# Client Demo Script

## Purpose

Use this script when presenting the system to a supermarket owner you want to sell to.

This is not a technical walkthrough. It is a guided business demo.

The goal is to help the client quickly see:

- how sales become faster
- how stock is controlled better
- how credit and supplier balances are followed up
- how the owner gets a clearer picture of the business than with the old Access workflow

## Before The Demo

1. Confirm the app is running well.
2. Confirm the public/ngrok link is active.
3. Use the owner's assigned accounts only.
4. Make sure sample data looks clean and realistic.
5. Avoid showing internal setup unless the client asks.

## Recommended Demo Flow

### 1. Start With The Owner View

Show:

- Dashboard
- summary cards
- recent sales or activity

Say:

- this screen gives you a quick view of what is happening in the business
- you can quickly see sales, low stock, credit follow-up, and other important numbers

### 2. Show The Sales Desk

Show:

- sales entry screen
- product search
- customer selection
- totals area

Do:

- add a few products
- change quantity
- choose a customer
- save one cash sale
- print the receipt

Say:

- the cashier does not need to struggle with many separate screens
- the system calculates totals quickly and keeps the workflow simple
- receipts are ready immediately after the sale

### 3. Show Credit Sale And Payment Follow-Up

Show:

- one credit sale
- customer payment screen
- customer statement

Say:

- the system helps you see who owes you and what has been paid
- this reduces forgotten balances and improves follow-up

### 4. Show Purchase And Stock Control

Show:

- purchase entry
- stock balances
- stock movement history

Do:

- enter one supplier purchase
- confirm stock increased

Say:

- once stock is received, the balance updates in the system
- you can track what entered, what sold, and what remains

### 5. Show Management Reports

Show:

- customer statement
- supplier statement
- aging reports
- stock balance or low stock report

Say:

- this is where the owner gets control and visibility
- instead of checking many manual records, the reports explain the business quickly

### 6. Show Returns Or Stock Corrections

Show:

- sale return flow or stock adjustment

Say:

- the system also helps correct mistakes in a controlled way
- this improves accountability compared to manual changes

## Main Selling Points

- browser-based and easier to access than old Access screens
- faster cashier workflow
- stronger stock control
- better customer credit follow-up
- clearer supplier balance tracking
- ready printouts for receipts, invoices, and statements
- role-based access for different staff
- cleaner reports for owner decision-making

## Questions To Ask During The Demo

Use these questions to keep the client talking:

1. does this sales flow feel faster than what you use now?
2. would your cashier manage this after short training?
3. are these stock and credit reports useful for your daily work?
4. what part feels most valuable to your business?
5. what would you still want improved before real use?

## End The Demo Well

Close with:

- what they liked most
- what they would want adjusted
- whether they want a second guided session
- whether they want a quotation or next-step discussion
